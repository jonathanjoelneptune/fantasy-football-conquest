The Chronicle of Six Battles

The complete six-matchup chronicle is the central historical record of the week. Each issue is told as part of the surviving account of how Mount Olympus was ultimately conquered, even while the reader experiences every victory, reversal and surprise along the road.

As the campaign advances, the paper itself ages with the war. Later chapters gain deeper staining and darker edges, turning the season into a visual history as well as a statistical one.
