The Chronicle • Blood, Fortune & Fate

Week 1 did not pass gently into history. It arrived with pride, panic, impossible escapes, and the kind of heartbreak that makes a manager stare at a lineup long after the final whistle. By the time the last points were counted, six battles had taken their price and the road toward Mount Olympus had narrowed again.

Hercules Unchained broke Aries It Out open in a 55.6-point rout. By the end, the fight had stopped being a contest and become a warning to every army watching.

The Peasants of Troy escaped Winging It by only 3.8 points. This was not conquest so much as survival: one last blade between victory and a week of regret.

🏺Hades Nutz broke TroyisBetterthanOdyssey open in a 49.6-point rout. By the end, the fight had stopped being a contest and become a warning to every army watching.

Winged Victory Formation escaped The Beer of Olympus by only 0.6 points. This was not conquest so much as survival: one last blade between victory and a week of regret.

Apollo's Archers defeated Flight of JarYES, 133.1 to 110.3. The margin was firm enough to matter, but the defeated side fought long enough to leave scars.

BeginnersRuck escaped Uranus Colonizer by only 2.5 points. This was not conquest so much as survival: one last blade between victory and a week of regret.

Then the gods intervened. Caleb Williams erupted for 37.3 points for Apollo's Archers, a legendary performance worthy of the Hall of Living Legends. Josh Allen (35.7), Derrick Henry (34.8), Kenneth Walker (32.6) joined the procession, turning ordinary matchups into stories managers will still remember years from now.

The week carried both mercy and cruelty. Winged Victory Formation survived the closest judgment of the slate, while Hercules Unchained authored its most merciless victory. Above them all, Hercules Unchained posted 139.3 points against a league average of 114.6, the loudest banner on the field.

When the fires finally dimmed, there were no untouched armies. Some left Week 1 believing the gods had chosen them. Others left convinced fate had cheated them. Both are probably wrong. The season is not finished with any of them yet.
