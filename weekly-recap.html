The Chronicle of Six Battles

The complete six-matchup chronicle belongs here as the central historical record of the week. Each issue is written from the perspective of a historian who already knows that one army eventually conquered Olympus, while the reader experiences every victory, reversal, rivalry and surprise along the road to that ending.

As the season advances, the paper itself ages with the war. Later chapters gain deeper staining, darker edges and a more battle-worn character, turning the full season into a visual history as well as a statistical one.
