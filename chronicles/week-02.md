# The Chronicle of Week 2 — The Wars Within

*Before Olympus could be conquered, its armies first turned their weapons upon their own.*

Week 1 had drawn the first blood.

Week 2 drew different battle lines.

For the first time in the young campaign, the armies of Olympus did not march across the continent in search of distant enemies. The West turned upon the West. The East turned upon the East. Allies in geography became enemies on the battlefield, and by the end of the week six more territories would change hands.

Some armies announced themselves with overwhelming force.

Others survived by fractions.

And one battle would be decided by **thirty-four hundredths of a point**.

## The Peasants of Troy vs. Hercules Unchained

### Thirty-Four Hundredths

**Hercules Unchained 91.86 • The Peasants of Troy 91.52**

There are defeats that leave no room for argument.

This was not one of them.

The Peasants of Troy and Hercules Unchained fought one of the strangest battles of the young war. Neither army reached 100 points. Neither managed to seize control. And when everything was finally counted, the difference between victory and defeat was **0.34 points**.

New England's defense nearly carried Troy across the line by itself, producing **23 points**, the finest defensive performance anywhere in Olympus that week.

Hercules answered with Jahmyr Gibbs and his **20.3 points**, while Minnesota's defense added another 18.

Then the numbers stopped moving.

**91.86–91.52.**

For the second consecutive week, the Peasants of Troy found themselves in a battle decided by less than four points. In Week 1, they had escaped.

This time, fate collected its debt.

**Demeter's Harvest Fields fell to Hercules Unchained**, and the newcomers had done something considerably more important than produce another spectacular score. After two battles in their first campaign, Hercules remained unbeaten.

The Peasants had survived their first brush with the sword's edge.

They did not survive the second.

## TroyisBetterthanOdyssey vs. Aries It Out

### The Fury of Troy

**TroyisBetterthanOdyssey 146.46 • Aries It Out 102.78**

One week earlier, TroyisBetterthanOdyssey had been swallowed by Hades.

Then came the answer.

Dak Prescott delivered **29.8 points**. CeeDee Lamb erupted for **31.3**. Davante Adams added **35.5**.

Together, Troy's army produced **146.46 points**, the greatest score of Week 2 and a staggering **58.76-point improvement** from its opening defeat.

Aries It Out did not go quietly. Jaxon Smith-Njigba produced **38.0 points**, one of the finest individual performances of the week, while Matthew Stafford added 28.

It hardly mattered.

The final margin was **43.68 points**, the greatest slaughter of Week 2.

**Ares' Warroom fell to TroyisBetterthanOdyssey.**

And somewhere else on the western front, Joey Neptune's Peasants had just lost by 0.34.

Thus Week 2 produced a particularly cruel bit of household symmetry: one Neptune army suffered the week's greatest heartbreak while the other rose as its **High King**.

## 🏺Hades Nutz vs. Winging It

### The Underworld Marches On

**🏺Hades Nutz 109.92 • Winging It 86.00**

Hades Nutz had opened the season with destruction.

Week 2 brought something different: control.

Josh Allen erupted for **40.82 points**, the highest individual score recorded anywhere in Olympus that week. It was his second consecutive appearance among the great performances of the young season, and this time he crossed the forty-point threshold.

Winging It never found an equal response. DeVonta Smith delivered 22.7. George Kittle added 16.0. But Puka Nacua produced nothing, and Jaxson Dart managed only 0.8.

The final judgment was **109.92–86.00**.

**Aphrodite's Garden fell to Hades Nutz.**

There was no 49-point massacre this time. There did not need to be.

Two weeks had passed.

Hades had won them both.

## Winged Victory Formation vs. Apollo's Archers

### A House Divided

**Apollo's Archers 92.52 • Winged Victory Formation 63.50**

Some wars divide kingdoms.

This one divided a household.

Courtney Quintero's Winged Victory Formation met Mike Quintero's Apollo's Archers with family bragging rights sitting somewhere beside the territory at stake.

The battle itself proved considerably less romantic.

Winged Victory could never find its army. Saquon Barkley scored 2.5. DJ Moore finished at **-0.1**. Baltimore's defense added only 3.

Apollo did not need greatness to capitalize. Ja'Marr Chase supplied **23.0 points**, Derrick Henry added 16.2, and Brandon Aubrey contributed 17 from the kicker position.

Apollo won **92.52–63.50**.

The 156.02 combined points made it the week's **Rock Fight**, while Winged Victory's 63.50 became the lowest score produced by any army in Week 2.

**Heart of the Labyrinth fell to Apollo's Archers.**

The Quintero household remained intact.

The same could not be said for the battlefield.

## Beer Goggles vs. BeginnersRuck

### The Champion Answers

**Beer Goggles 95.98 • BeginnersRuck 91.56**

The reigning champion had begun the new Odyssey with a defeat by six-tenths of a point.

Week 2 offered no easy recovery.

BeginnersRuck, playing the second fantasy football matchup of his life, refused to behave like an inexperienced army. Amon-Ra St. Brown erupted for **30.7 points**. Travis Kelce added 20.6. Jalen Hurts supplied another 18.2.

Beer Goggles answered with balance.

Kenneth Walker scored 20.8. James Cook added 20.4. Christian Watson contributed 12.1.

Neither army reached 100.

But champions do not receive extra victories for style.

**Beer Goggles 95.98. BeginnersRuck 91.56.**

A difference of **4.42 points** was enough.

**Zeus' Sky Temple fell to Beer Goggles**, and the defending champion had finally planted its first victorious banner of the new campaign.

BeginnersRuck had now experienced both sides of the narrow finish: a 2.46-point victory in the first fantasy matchup of his life, followed immediately by a 4.42-point defeat.

Olympus was proving an efficient teacher.

## Flight of JarYES vs. Uranus Colonizer

### The Best Losing Army

**Flight of JarYES 110.24 • Uranus Colonizer 106.58**

Uranus Colonizer did almost everything required to win.

Patrick Mahomes scored **29.0**. Christian McCaffrey delivered 20.6. Chris Olave added 18.6.

Uranus finished with **106.58 points**, the fourth-highest score among all twelve armies in Week 2.

There was one problem.

Flight of JarYES scored more.

Jonathan Taylor led the charge with **27.2 points**. Omarion Hampton added 16.5. Jayden Daniels, Garrett Wilson and Trey McBride all crossed 14.

The result was another western-edge finish on the eastern battlefield:

**110.24–106.58.**

Uranus would have defeated seven of the other ten possible opponents that week.

Instead, it lost.

**Tartarus fell to Flight of JarYES**, while Uranus Colonizer earned the less desirable distinction of becoming Week 2's **Robbed** army.

Such was Olympus.

Sometimes strength determined history.

Sometimes the schedule did.

## When Olympus Turned Upon Itself

Week 2 had begun with twelve armies divided between two coasts.

It ended with six civil wars written into the map.

**Demeter's Harvest Fields** fell to Hercules Unchained. **Ares' Warroom** fell to TroyisBetterthanOdyssey. **Aphrodite's Garden** fell to Hades Nutz. **Heart of the Labyrinth** fell to Apollo's Archers. **Zeus' Sky Temple** fell to Beer Goggles. **Tartarus** fell to Flight of JarYES.

But the scoreboard told an even stranger story.

TroyisBetterthanOdyssey went from 87.70 points and defeat to **146.46 and the High King's crown**.

Hercules Unchained went from the highest score in Olympus to just 91.86, yet somehow emerged from both weeks with the same thing: victory.

The defending champion Beer Goggles finally answered its opening heartbreak.

BeginnersRuck discovered the other side of a close finish.

Apollo's Archers won the first household war of the Odyssey.

And the Peasants of Troy learned that the distance between conquest and defeat could be measured in hundredths.

Above them all stood **Josh Allen**, whose **40.82 points** became the first 40-point performance of the new campaign and another entry in the Hall of Living Legends. Jaxon Smith-Njigba followed with 38.0, Davante Adams with 35.5, CeeDee Lamb with 31.3, and Amon-Ra St. Brown with 30.7.

Two weeks were now written.

That was still far too little to know who would ultimately conquer Mount Olympus.

But it was enough for the first differences between these armies to begin emerging.

Some had already learned how to dominate.

Some had learned how to survive.

Some had learned how quickly fortune could reverse.

And some were beginning to discover that the road to Olympus would not always reward the army that fought the best battle.

**The first wars had introduced the armies.**

**The wars within had begun to reveal them.**
